About Dr. Gauri & Garbhvedam

Welcome to Garbhvedam Online Garbhsanskar Classes, founded and personally guided by Dr. Gauri Ramesh Hukkeri [M.D. Balrog (बालरोगतज्ञ), शास्त्रोक्त गर्भसंस्कार तज्ञ, व योग उपासक].

Garbhvedam bridges ancient Ayurvedic Vedic wisdom with modern pediatric and obstetric sciences. Through scientific Garbhsanskar, targeted yoga, trimester-wise nutrition, and pre-conception counseling, we guide aspiring and expectant mothers toward delivering happy, healthy, and spiritually grounded babies.
